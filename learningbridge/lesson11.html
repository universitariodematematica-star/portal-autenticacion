My Family

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can name family members and say who they are.

Grammar Formula

This + is + my + family member
This is my mother.
This is my father.
My + noun + is + complement
My family is important.

Key Vocabulary
family mother father sister brother grandmother grandfather parents home respect
Grammar in Context

Use This is my... to introduce one family member.
Use my to show that something or someone belongs to you.
Use family words to talk about people at home.

Before Reading

What people are important in a family? What family traditions do you know?

Culture Reading

Family in Latin America

Family is very important in many Latin American communities. A family can be small or large. It can include a mother, a father, brothers, sisters, grandparents, cousins, uncles, aunts, and other important people. Family is not only about living in the same house. It is also about care, respect, help, and shared memories.

In many homes, family members spend time together during meals, celebrations, religious events, school activities, and community gatherings. A grandmother can tell stories. A grandfather can give advice. Parents can help children study. Brothers and sisters can play, talk, and learn together.

Family traditions are different in every country and every home. In Ecuador, Colombia, Peru, Mexico, and other countries, families can celebrate birthdays, holidays, local festivals, and special Sundays together. Food, music, stories, and respect are often part of family life.

In English, students can introduce family members with simple sentences: "This is my mother." "This is my brother." "My family is important." These sentences help students talk about people they love and respect.
