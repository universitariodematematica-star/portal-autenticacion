My Family

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning
A · LEARNING GOAL B · VOCABULARY C · GRAMMAR D · READING E · PRACTICE

A. Learning Goal

Students identify and describe family members using basic English vocabulary and simple sentences.

B. Key Vocabulary

  • mother
  • father
  • brother
  • sister
  • grandmother
  • grandfather
  • family

Grammar Formula

This is my + family member.
This is my mother.

Grammar in Context

Use my before a family member to show possession.

Example: This is my brother.

C. My Family

My name is Ana. I live with my family. I have a mother, a father, and two brothers.

My mother is a teacher. My father is a doctor. My brothers are students.

We like spending time together.

D. Reading Check

1. What is the girl's name?
2. Who is a teacher?
3. How many brothers does Ana have?
